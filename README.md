#  DayTradingToolkit 📈
Official GitHub home for DayTradingToolkit.com. Providing research-backed trading education, technical analysis tools, and frameworks for disciplined retail trading.

### Empowering Retail Traders through Data-Driven Education and Transparency.

DayTradingToolkit is a professional educational hub dedicated to bridge the gap between institutional market knowledge and retail trading. We focus on evidence-based strategies, trading psychology, and technical analysis.

---

## 🏛️ Our Mission
We combat the "gamification" of trading by providing research-backed content. As cited in our publications, we prioritize peer-reviewed academic journals (e.g., *Management Science*, *Yale Law Journal*) to ensure our community receives verified financial intelligence.

## 🛠️ Core Focus Areas
- **Trading Psychology:** Managing the cognitive biases of social media and FOMO.
- **Risk Management:** Advanced position sizing and capital preservation frameworks.
- **Technical Analysis:** High-probability setups using institutional-grade indicators.
