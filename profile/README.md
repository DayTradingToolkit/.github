DayTradingToolkit is an independent FinTech research and education initiative. In an industry full of hype, paid signals, and "guaranteed" win rates, we deliver **honest, data-backed education** built by an active trader and full-stack developer.

We translate complex market concepts, academic research, and real screen-time experience into actionable frameworks for retail traders — without paywalls, fake promises, or ghost-written content.
